# Product definition

FANTASY HUD is a portfolio-management and command-center interface for serious fantasy-football players. Sleeper is where users play; FANTASY HUD is where users manage all of their teams as one portfolio.

The initial target is high-volume Sleeper best-ball users.

## Future value

- All teams in one place
- Player exposure
- NFL-team exposure
- Draft-capital allocation
- Stacks and co-holdings
- Draft history
- Weekly scores and matchups

## Non-goals

- News
- Rankings
- Start/sit advice
- Waiver recommendations
- Trade calculator
- Betting
- Social feed
